New York unclaimed-property reporting

Thursday, December 10, 2026: first-class mail.

Sunday, January 10, 2027: certified mail, only above $1,000. Wednesday, March 10, 2027: the report. January 10, 2027 is a Sunday. No OSC weekend-move rule was found, so that date stays a Sunday.

Thursday, December 10, 2026 First-class owner mail.
Sunday, January 10, 2027 Certified mail, only when the item is above $1,000. The date is a Sunday. No OSC weekend-move rule was found.
Wednesday, March 10, 2027 Report date. The file is a draft the holder reviews, signs, and uploads. We do not remit.

Two letter cases

  • At or under $1,000: first-class mail only. Certified mail is not part of this case.
  • Over $1,000: first-class mail, and certified mail on Sunday, January 10, 2027.

Source: New York State Comptroller, Reporting Unclaimed Funds to New York State and Due Diligence for Companies Holding Abandoned Funds.
